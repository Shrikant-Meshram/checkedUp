# Help Manual

## Patient

### Book a Test
1. Go to **Tests** or **Packages** and click **Book**.
2. Enter your name, age, gender, and phone number.
3. Enter your address and pincode. Set your location on the map if you can.
4. Pick a date and time slot.
5. Check the summary and confirm.
6. You will get a booking ID and a WhatsApp message.

### My Bookings
1. Go to **My Bookings** in the sidebar.
2. See your booking status, lab, and payment details.
3. Click a booking to view full details.
4. Cancel or reschedule if needed (give a reason).

### Payment
Pay the lab assistant at your door. You can pay by UPI or cash.

### My Reports
1. Go to **My Reports** in the sidebar.
2. View or download your report when it is ready.
3. You will also get a WhatsApp message when the report is ready.

## Admin

### Manage Tests & Packages
1. Go to **Tests** or **Packages**.
2. Click **Create** to add a new test or package.
3. Use the edit icon to change details.
4. Use delete to remove one.

### Manage Lab Owners
1. Go to **Lab Owners**.
2. Click **Add Lab Owner**.
3. Fill lab details, address, and location.
4. Upload the required documents.

### Manage Bookings
1. Go to **Bookings**.
2. Use filters to find a booking.
3. Assign a lab using the edit icon.
4. You can also view details and download reports.

> Once the lab assistant reaches the patient, the lab cannot be changed.

### Payments
1. Go to **Payments** to see all payments.
2. Filter by UPI or cash, date, or status.
3. Open **Payment Settings** to update the UPI QR code.

### Settings
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. **Roles**: manage user permissions.
3. **Categories**: manage test categories.
4. **Commission**: set how commission is split.

### Settlements
1. Go to **Settlements**.
2. Select completed paid bookings.
3. Click **Send** and enter UTR number and bank name.
4. For multiple bookings of the same lab, use bulk send.
5. Export history when needed.

## Lab Owner

### Assign Assistants
1. Go to **Bookings**.
2. Find a booking with status Pending or Assigned.
3. Use the **Assign Assistant** dropdown to pick your assistant.

> Only assistants from your lab can be assigned.

### Manage Assistants
1. Go to **Assistants**.
2. Click **Add Assistant**.
3. Enter name, email, phone, and password.
4. Upload the ID proof document.
5. View, edit, or delete assistants from the list.

### Upload Reports
1. Go to **Bookings** or **Upload Reports**.
2. Find a booking where sample is collected and payment is done.
3. Click **Upload Report** and select the file.
4. The patient will get a WhatsApp message.

### Payments
1. Go to **Payments** to see payments for your lab.
2. See commission and lab share for each payment.

### Settlements
1. Go to **Settlements**.
2. See bookings ready for settlement.
3. When admin sends payment, click **Verify** to confirm.
4. View and export settlement history.

## Lab Assistant

### Your Daily Work
Follow these steps for each home visit:
1. Open **Bookings** to see your assigned work.
2. Navigate to the patient using the map.
3. Click **Mark Reached** when you arrive.
4. If patient wants extra tests, use **Add Tests**.
5. Collect the sample. Take at least 1 photo of the sample.
6. Collect payment by UPI or cash.

### Collect Payment
1. Open the booking and click **Collect Payment**.
2. Choose **UPI** or **Cash**.
3. For UPI: show the QR code and upload the payment screenshot.
4. For Cash: no receipt needed.
5. Submit to confirm.

### What Each Status Means
| Status | What To Do |
|---|---|
| Assigned | Go to the patient |
| Reached | You have arrived. Collect sample next. |
| Sample Collected | Collect payment |
| Processing | Wait for lab to finish |
| Report Ready / Completed | Work is done |
